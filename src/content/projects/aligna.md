description:
  - >-
    Every backpack hangs its load a few inches behind your spine, and your body
    fixes that the only way it can: shoulders roll forward, head drifts out,
    lower back rounds. ALIGNA starts from the back panel and designs the bag
    around it — three contoured zones, a gentle forward curve at the upper
    back, relief over the thoracic spine, and the deepest curve with the
    thickest padding at the lumbar, over a thin semi-rigid sheet that gives
    about half a centimetre and springs back.
  - >-
    The straps work as a system. Shoulder straps pull the load in toward the
    body, a low-mounted chest strap draws the shoulders back and down, and the
    lumbar curve pushes forward from behind, so the weight sits within an inch
    of the spine. 600D shell, all-metal hardware, sized to a 16" MacBook Pro.
    Design and tech pack were finished in August 2026 and pre-production
    samples exist in three colours. The Kickstarter campaign is in
    preparation; the prelaunch site is collecting the list.
